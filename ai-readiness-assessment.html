Strategic Recommendations

  • Start with Foundation: Prioritize data integration and conversation intelligence in Phase 1 to establish the groundwork for advanced AI capabilities.
  • Quick Wins: Call transcription and automated dashboards deliver immediate value and build organizational confidence in AI investments.
  • Scale Thoughtfully: Expand only after validating pilots. Use success metrics from Phase 1 to inform Phase 2 decisions.
  • Change Management: Invest 15-20% of budget in training, communication, and adoption programs to ensure user buy-in.
  • Measure Everything: Establish baseline KPIs before implementation and track progress monthly to demonstrate value.
  • Governance First: Create AI ethics guidelines and data privacy protocols before deploying customer-facing AI.
