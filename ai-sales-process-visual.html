⚖️ Old Way vs. AI-Enabled Way

Sales Discipline Manual Process AI-Enabled Process
1. Call Reports Reps type notes, managers review text Calls transcribed → key moments flagged → summary generated
2. Calendar Planning Scheduling and routing done by intuition AI suggests optimal routes and visit priorities
3. Sales Funnel Management Gut-feel forecasts and subjective deal updates Auto-updates based on engagement; predicts stall risk
4. Top 5 Prospects Reps pick prospects based on instinct System ranks prospects by probability and urgency
5. Sales Dashboard Lagging indicators only, reviewed monthly Real-time metrics with root cause analysis
6. Results Confirmation Verbal reviews of performance Automatically attributes wins/losses to behaviors
7. Customer 360° Files Data scattered across systems Unified, auto-compiled account view with wallet share
8. Looking Forward / Back Narrative summaries and goals Pattern recognition → goal recommendations
9. Travel Days & Coaching Feedback based on memory Coaching guided by AI-identified skill gaps
10. Monthly Performance Reactive and lagging Predictive assessments with alerts
